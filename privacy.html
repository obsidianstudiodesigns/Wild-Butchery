Privacy policy

Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). Last updated November 2026.

Wild Butchery ("we", "us") is a butchery in the Bush Centre, Marloth Park. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit this website, message us or place an order, why we need it, and the rights you have under POPIA.

1. Who is responsible

The responsible party is Wild Butchery, Bush Centre, 3883 Olifant Road, Marloth Park, 1321, Mpumalanga. You can reach us on 064 072 5121 (WhatsApp), 071 658 3316 or wildbutchery@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Your name and phone or WhatsApp number when you message or call us.
  • Your email address if you email us.
  • Order details, such as the products and quantities you ask for and the day you want to collect.
  • Records of payments made in the shop, as required for our accounting.

This website does not take payments and never asks for card or banking details.

3. How the braai planner works

The braai planner works out suggested amounts in your own browser and does not store anything on a server. When you press "Send list on WhatsApp", your browser opens WhatsApp with the list filled in, and nothing reaches us until you send it yourself. Messages sent on WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

4. Why we use it

  • To answer your questions and prepare and confirm your order.
  • To let you know when your order is ready or if something is out of stock.
  • To keep the accounting and tax records the law requires.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we will not add you to marketing lists or broadcast groups without your consent.

5. Who we share it with

We share your information only where it is needed to run the business or where the law requires it, for example with our accountant, our payment and banking providers, and the messaging and email services we use to talk to you.

6. How long we keep it

Order messages are kept only as long as they are useful for your order and any follow-up questions. Financial records are kept for as long as tax law requires (currently five years).

7. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including password-protected phones, accounts and backups.

8. This website

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking analytics, and it does not ask you to log in. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service secure. The map on the home page is provided by Google Maps, which may set its own cookies when it loads. Links to WhatsApp, Facebook, Google Maps and email open services with their own privacy policies.

9. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, and withdraw consent you have given. Contact us using the details above. If you are not happy with our response you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
