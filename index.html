What goes on the fire?

    A rough guide of about 350 g per adult and half that per child, per braai. We'll fine-tune it with you at the counter. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.

    Good to know

    What is C-grade meat?

    C-grade beef comes from older animals and has a yellow fat cover, usually from a life on grass. It is a little firmer than A-grade, carries more flavour and costs less, which makes it a favourite for stews, potjiekos and a slow braai. Our specials often include C-grade T-bone, club steak and stewing beef.

    Do you sell game meat?

    Yes. Game boerewors and game sosaties are regulars, and we often have other game products depending on the season. Send us a WhatsApp to ask what's in today.

    Can I order before I arrive in Marloth Park?

    Send your list to 064 072 5121 on WhatsApp with the day you'd like to collect, and we'll confirm what we can have ready. The braai planner above writes the message for you.

    Where do I find your specials?

    We post specials and any changes to our trading days on our Facebook page. Specials run while stocks last.
